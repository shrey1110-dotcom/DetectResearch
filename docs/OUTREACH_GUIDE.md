# Academic Outreach Email Best Practices

## Structure of an Effective Cold Email to a Professor
1. **Clear Subject Line**: Include specific topic and interest (e.g. `Undergraduate Interest in Your Research on Bioelectronics`).
2. **Personalized Salutation**: Address professor formally (`Dear Professor [Last Name],`).
3. **Introduction**: State your major, academic year, and university affiliation.
4. **Specific Reference**: Mention a specific paper or project from their lab and why it caught your attention.
5. **Relevant Experience**: Highlight relevant coursework, skills, or class projects.
6. **Polite Call to Action**: Ask about potential undergraduate opportunities or learning paths.
